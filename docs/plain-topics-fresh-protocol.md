# Career and marriage plain-reading review

These five public cases were chosen before running the new presentation, for different careers, places and relationship histories; no case is in the earlier repository fixtures. They are a small convenience sample, not a representative or blinded test. The fixture records birth-data sources and qualifications as well as independent biographical references. Reported birth times—including AA entries—are not independently verified. Kiran Bedi's time is C-rated, and Oprah Winfrey's is recalled and disputed. Van der Waals's marriage year differs between cited sources.

For each chart, compute the ordinary Nadi reading using its recorded birth time, Lahiri and mean nodes. Capture the previous Overview headline and the new plain career and marriage wording, the selected current/next Nadi windows and their natal-planet explanations, plus the corresponding full practitioner findings. Check that the new wording draws only on the calculation, distinguishes marriage indications from guarantees, and does not turn a long-term partnership into a legal marriage. Compare career themes to the documented occupation and record misses and excessively broad lists as well as apparent matches. Do not feed known outcomes into the text builder or tune it to these people.

For the dated events listed before running the review, also recreate the topic card 90 days before the published event and check whether any of its selected Nadi passages overlaps the event's local calendar day. Repeat at dates shifted by 180 days in each direction, using the same 90-day lead. The fixture records the event zone when known; an unknown zone falls back to the chart's birth zone. The event's clock is unknown, so an overlap with any part of the day is only a possible overlap. The shifted dates are comparison dates, not verified non-events, and long passages may cover both. Events known only by year, and the disputed marriage year, are not assigned invented days. Do not treat an engagement or partnership as a legal marriage.

These are checks of presentation and implementation, with a small descriptive retrospective timing check. Whether a list mentions an occupation does not show predictive accuracy; being able to explain a rule does not make it empirically reliable. The cases and events are not blind or representative, and no event date is used to generate its own chart. No user comprehension trial or independent practitioner review is implied. A personal chart is checked separately in the user's private workspace and is not part of the public fixture or results.

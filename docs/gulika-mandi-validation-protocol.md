# Gulika: fixed public-case check

The initial protocol was written before computing placements for its four cases. It checks one
specific literal interpretation, not the whole Prasna Marga system, and it does not tune any rule
to the results.

examining their Gulika placements. They are fresh to this repository, but are not a blind or random
sample. All are known parents; no non-parents were selected, so the sample cannot estimate
sensitivity, specificity or predictive performance. Astro-Databank's AA rating reflects a quoted

## Fixed assessment

Use Lahiri, mean node, the app's refracted upper-limb sunrise, the published coordinates and the
stated fixed civil offsets. Calculate the Kerala weekday-constant Gulika and count natal houses
by whole sign. At each reported time and at −15, −5, +5 and +15 minutes, record the placement.
This probes the consequence of a rounded or uncertain time; it does not establish the true time.

Prasna Marga 14.67–70 includes an adverse statement about offspring when Gulika is in the fifth.
For these known parents, a fifth-house placement is a counterexample **to a literal standalone
claim of childlessness**. The absence of fifth-house Gulika does not confirm the rule, nor does
this check test other combinations, birth timing, horary questions or alternative conventions.
If the sensitive window includes both the fifth and another house, the counterexample is
time-sensitive. Report every case, including untriggered results. The results are in
[gulika-mandi-review.md](gulika-mandi-review.md); regenerate with
`node --import tsx scripts/eval-gulika-cases.ts`.

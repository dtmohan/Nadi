# ALP marriage and matched KP comparison: prospective analysis plan

Written before the fixture was scored. The available ALP volume 2's marriage example identifies a pada-navamsa relation, and ch. 25 names the seventh lords from natal and progressed lagnas and gives a literal dasa/bhukti example. It does not define all forms of planetary connection or a complete natal promise. The program therefore reports: (a) natal seventh house/lord and D9 facts, with BPHS 18.1's distinct sign-level spouse-happiness clue; (b) the original partial ALP pada clue; (c) the exact two-lord period pair from the example, unordered; and (d) whether the seventh lords are identical or share a natal sign. It does not count these as independent votes or say the pair/absence is necessary. The period-pair choice is a narrow app operationalisation, and the use of Akshaya rasi's Moon-based dasa/bhukti is the current ALP convention. The published example does not settle whether other contacts or other period conventions suffice.

The additional KP comparison keeps all events together and shifts each local calendar date by the same nonzero integer offset with absolute value 180–365 days, retaining its clock and zone. Randomly shuffle valid offsets reproducibly and sample at most 200 without replacement. Reject dates beyond the judgment instant, after recorded death, before the candidate birth instant, with nonexistent or ambiguous local clocks, coinciding with a known same-type event, or reversing actual event chronology. Require at least 40 eligible offsets. This preserves event order and approximate age, but not every life-stage constraint, event season, or true non-event status. Show KP's natal cusp separately from the date-dependent period hits: a constant natal point cannot distinguish dates. Compare the same KP period, transit, qualified and Aurora rules on actual and shifted dates. Report available offsets and flat distributions; do not use percentages as calibrated probabilities. The older independent-date comparison remains separately visible for context.

Use the four people in `scripts/fixtures/alp-kp-matched-fresh.json`, selected from public birth and dated event records before inspecting results, excluding anyone used in earlier fixture files. Run Lahiri, mean nodes, and the specified clock basis. Record ALP context per wedding and KP matched versus legacy baselines at each reported birth anchor. Separately scan ±5 minutes with matched comparisons and check Validate at the ordinary-segment midpoint for exactly equal outputs. Test Cindy Crawford's documented alternative 02:08 separately and Flore Landru's genealogy-reported September 29 birth date separately; neither is selected according to score. Treat the twins/deliveries once each. The cohort includes a single-event case and an 1800s local-mean-time case. Sources and uncertainty are recorded in the fixture; no actual birth certificates were inspected. Event clocks not given in a source are noon in a stated or assumed zone. Chronology controls are correlated with each other and some events may overlap actual circumstances. No population sampling, preregistered external trial, or predictive accuracy claim is implied.

A separate personal case is evaluated outside the repository using a family-event subset and a broader selection. No personal chart inputs, events, or results are included in the public fixture, evaluation script, or result artifact. Report disagreements, unavailable comparisons, and ties; do not pick a time from the best raw score. Astrology is not established as a reliable way to infer a birth time.

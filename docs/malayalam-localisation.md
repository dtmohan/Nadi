# Malayalam localisation: first slice

Malayalam is an incremental translation, not yet a complete translated reading. The language choice is available throughout the app and is stored on the device. The first slice covers the entry flow, navigation, birth-chart identity, Nadi overview and selected career and marriage wording. Untranslated details, classical sources, reports and PDFs remain in English; the interface says so. The translations need review by a fluent Malayalam reader familiar with the terminology.

The chart's nine planet names, twelve signs and 27 birth stars have Malayalam labels. The localised overview is assembled from the same structured chart data, topic selection, age gates and synthesis as the English view. It does not calculate a separate verdict. Where a sentence has not been translated, keep the original English instead of guessing, and mark the language of that fallback for assistive technology. Do not send birth details or reading text to a translation service.

## Terminology

Keep house lord **ഭാവാധിപൻ**, sign lord **രാശ്യാധിപൻ**, and star lord **നക്ഷത്രാധിപൻ** distinct. The requested **സഹാധിപൻ (sub-lord)** is retained with the English term because it may not be the terminology every KP reader uses. Randomly selected comparison dates are **ക്രമരഹിതമായി തിരഞ്ഞെടുത്ത തീയതികൾ**. Verify technical wording in context as each school is translated; equivalent-looking terms are not necessarily interchangeable.

The bundled [Manjari](https://smc.org.in/fonts/manjari/) typeface is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Malayalam text uses that font and avoids tracking applied to English uppercase labels. English remains available at every stage.

# Chart-specific explanations

The plain reading connects an already evaluated chart fact to the source passage or life area that actually uses it. The presentation layer does not create an event score, a promise gate, a transit date, or a combined verdict across schools. Detailed source passages and technical tables remain available in practitioner mode.

- **Nadi:** Each displayed life-area theme carries the ID, source, participating planets and the recorded planet-to-planet connections of its own finding. Planet cards use the same age-gated life-area summaries. A named sign lord or star lord is a chart fact, not an automatically inferred result.
- **Parashari:** House cards use the selected house construction and show the house, its ruler and occupants alongside the already evaluated BPHS 11 and 24 and Phaladeepika 8 passages. Planet cards bring together the existing checks and selected house passages. The strength-band explanation identifies the app's provisional threshold rather than claiming that an individual result will occur in full.
- **KP:** The cusp and planet views use the KP ayanamsa and Placidus houses. The four-step and six-step conventions are labelled separately. A node represents the houses owned by its agents; it is not described as owning those houses. Source findings retain their deciding or secondary status and their own scope. Mental-health and family outcomes are not inferred from an isolated rule.
- **Jaimini:** Planet and life-area views name the chart's movable significator and its D1 and D9 positions. The eight-karaka convention does not assign a movable role to Ketu. D9 is calculated from the same birth longitude and is not independent evidence.
- **ALP:** Houses and their rulers are counted from the selected progressed point, while the birth rising sign remains a separate reference. The source's past/present/future groups are shown without treating them as a calendar exclusion. A house-lord explanation is shown only when an already-fired rule explicitly checks that house's lord and has a reviewed topic paraphrase; the original source and matched conditions remain attached.
- **Divisions:** The division's matter and selected house readings come from the existing BPHS 7.1–8, M.S. Mehta *Analysing Horoscope Through Modern Techniques*, chapter XVIII, and Shivanshu Pande method metadata. Unnamed divisional houses are not given a fabricated interpretation. D2 and D30 follow their cited sign/segment methods rather than being treated as ordinary house charts. The score shown in the table is for the selected Vimsopaka scheme, not just the current division.
- **Prasna Marga:** The house paraphrases are bounded by the entered Raman translation, XIV.51–64. The fifth-house difficult passage names additional affliction conditions which the occupant check does not fully establish. The planetary condition proxy uses Nadi-derived effective dignity, solar contact and hemming and remains explicitly provisional. Natal and question-time charts remain separate.
- **Gochara:** The transit explanations use the natal Moon as the reference for Brihat Samhita 104 and Phaladeepika 26. Their house result, vedha and any applied modifiers remain separate from natal house meanings and from KP or Parashari event scores.

The explanations follow the existing birth-time, age and sensitive-content gates. They surface incomplete coverage and competing source indications rather than inventing a resolution. Source claims remain traditional interpretations, not empirical proof of a person's character or future.

# Fresh ALP marriage and sequence-preserving KP comparison

The [protocol](alp-kp-matched-protocol.md) and [four-person fixture](../scripts/fixtures/alp-kp-matched-fresh.json) were fixed before running the calculation. The [machine-readable results](alp-kp-matched-results.json) contain the full natal and timing context, alternative-source sensitivity checks, sampled offsets and file hashes. This is an exploratory, retrospective software and source-fidelity check on a small convenience sample; the natal records were not inspected, these dates are not a random population sample, and the comparison dates are not established non-events.

## ALP marriage observations

Across five weddings, the existing pada-navamsa clue was present **once** and the book's narrow exact natal/progressed-seventh-lord dasa/bhukti pair **zero times**, at the reported birth anchors. The separate BPHS 18.1 own/exaltation-sign spouse-happiness clue was absent in all four charts. The result does not mean marriage was denied: that BPHS clue concerns marriage quality, not a complete promise, and neither ALP timing check represents all possible connections or the full method.

## KP sequence-preserving comparison

The period-hit tally excludes the invariant natal cusp points, so the comparison tests the date-dependent part. All four anchor calculations had 200 sampled offsets out of 372 eligible offsets. The older independent-date totals, where available, include the natal cusp once per repeated matter/cusp/house combination; their numbers have a different scale.

For all four anchors the stricter Aurora v2 complete-chain count was 0, as was the mean of its shifted dates: no discrimination. Landru's 96% mid-rank describes this set of correlated offsets and does not correct for the selection of charts, rules or birth-time candidates. Her alternative birth date produces an 81% period-hit mid-rank; the result cannot resolve the source disagreement.

At the selectable midpoint of the reported-time segment in each ±5-minute scan, Validate and Rectify matched the entire sequence-preserving comparison. Each case used the same offset sets across its scanned segments. Topic readings and Validate also matched the ALP marriage context at the anchor, and Rectify and Validate matched at the segment midpoint. These tests establish consistency of implementation, not an empirical ability to infer birth time. The single-event case demonstrates that the new comparison is available where the older multi-event span comparison was not.

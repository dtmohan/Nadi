# Event-only full-day demonstration: protocol

This is a retrospective, non-blind demonstration, not fresh validation. Prince William and some of his events have appeared in the project's earlier Parashari fixtures; the reported birth time and related analyses were already accessible and seen. The candidate calculation will **not read or use a birth clock**, reference proximity, personal physical traits or the comparison source. Because the investigator knew the published birth time before coding, this must not be described as blinded. The subject was selected for a publicly timed birth and multiple well-documented events, not as a random or representative person. The three child events concern one family and are correlated.

## Case and evidence

The [event-only fixture](../scripts/fixtures/royal-event-only-case.json) records the birth date, birthplace, timezone and five event dates with at least two separately published sources each. Sources can repeat a single underlying palace announcement, so multiple reports must not be represented as independent witness or hospital verification. Wedding evidence uses the whole 11:00–12:15 service; no exact moment of vows is assumed. The initial selection uses the marriage, the first child's birth and the first air-ambulance work shift. The births of the other two children are held out from ranking. The +/-5-minute birth-clock and +/-30-minute work-shift intervals are stress scenarios, not verified uncertainty bounds. No year-only or vaguely dated event is used.

## Fixed calculation

Search the entire stated local civil birth date: midnight inclusive to the next local midnight exclusive. Use London civil time and daylight saving as provided by the timezone database; do not search adjacent dates. Use Nadi's Lahiri ayanamsa, mean node convention and 365.25-day Vimshottari year. Apply the existing partial source-linked Manley primary-house/house-lord/karaka checks in the relevant event divisional chart: D9 marriage, D7 childbirth, D10 job. D1 and additional clues are descriptive rather than a gate. Missing a primary link is unresolved in this partial method, not proof of an impossible event.

For each candidate and each input event, enumerate all main/sub/third Vimshottari paths encountered in that event's supplied clock interval. **First-stage support** means both main and subperiod have a relevant primary divisional link throughout the interval. **Third-stage support** additionally requires the third lord's link throughout the interval, for the same event. If links vary over the interval, report this ambiguity rather than claiming certainty. The chronology-derived intervals are either documented ceremony bounds or stated sensitivity scenarios; their edges are not guaranteed to contain the true physical event instant.

Rank the full day first by the number of input events with first-stage support. Retain all candidates tied at the maximum; among them rank by the number of events also satisfying the third stage, and retain every tie. If the first-stage maximum is zero, keep the full day unresolved. This lexicographic algorithm is a prespecified app convention, not Manley's complete procedure, a calibrated probability, or a guarantee of a birth time. Identical lords and related events are not independent confirmation. Do not use held-out events, birth-clock proximity, or later refinements to break ties.

Sample candidate births at five-second intervals, refining detected changes in the Boolean input and held-out evidence signatures to 200 ms. A feature starting and ending between samples can be missed. Include held-out signatures when segmenting solely so their duration coverage can be measured; they must not influence rank. Report the complete top sets, durations, input facts and duration-weighted held-out support alongside full-day baseline coverage. Do not rank by the fourth or fifth lords: the scan is not sufficiently fine and event clocks are uncertain.

After computing and saving the event-only results, compare every top range with the independently reported time in the contemporary [London Gazette birth notice](https://www.thegazette.co.uk/London/issue/49027/page/8215/data.pdf), also reported on the [Royal Family biography](https://www.royal.uk/the-prince-of-wales-0?page=4). Both derive from the same underlying royal announcement and are not independent medical measurements. Treat it as a well-documented comparison time, not infallible ground truth. Report whether it falls in the top set, whether the set touches its ±5-minute band, and the size of the entire set; do not tune the input events, weights, window or thresholds after comparison. Explain any discrepancy without assuming that the published time or the method is right.

Store the public-only inputs, report, machine-readable output and hashes. A separate unseen collection with a preregistered, genuinely blinded workflow and independently verified birth records would be needed to evaluate general accuracy.

# Conditional yoga, node and event combinations: fixed exploratory protocol

This protocol and `scripts/fixtures/parashari-conditional-fresh.json` were specified before running the fresh public cases or the personal comparison. The rules are source-adapted app conventions, not a validated predictive formula. Retain all outcomes, including ties and unassessed scenarios. Do not tune rules, event selection or weights to these results.

## Source and implementation

For Manley's five mapped topics, report D1 and the relevant division for each main, sub and third Vimshottari period. BPHS 34.11–15 describes conjunction, exchange and mutual full aspect for angle/trine lords, with qualifications for other lordships. Apply these formation criteria to the explicitly named rising-sign pairs in the existing BPHS table; a one-way aspect is visible but insufficient for this subset. Apply the natal table to divisional ascendants provisionally, not as a claim of an explicit BPHS divisional rule. Preserve each partner's house ownership and do not turn the pair into an independently favourable planet.

For a node, show its sign ruler, position and all house lordships. Record one-hop event links if the ruler occupies or fully aspects the event house, or has a direct conjunction/full-aspect relationship with the house lord or karaka. Direct identity with a house lord or karaka is already shown under the node's own links and is not re-added as an indirect link. Do not recursively transfer the ruler's labels, assume good or bad outcome, cast node aspects or promote the indirect links into the older primary gates. Manley's worked examples, *Birth Time Rectification* (2022), capture pp. 69–72 and 165, include the node ruler in the seventh or aspected by the seventh lord; pp. 140–141 describe the role of dispositors and aspects. BPHS 34.16 discusses nodes' house occupancy and conjunction, not a general one-hop dispositor scoring formula.

An event combination means the period lord occupies the event house and directly is, conjoins or fully aspects/is aspected by that house lord or karaka, in that same chart. Do not count a node's being in the sign of that house lord as a second factor: it is tautological. This narrower operationalization follows Manley's examples of a childbirth lord in the D7 fifth and connected to the fifth lord or Jupiter (pp. 69–70, 127–131). The same source discusses multiple possible ways to account for an event; this is not exhaustive or a claim that other combinations are invalid. The three kinds of clue may overlap.

Sources: [BPHS English translation, chapter 34](https://vedic-astro.s3.amazonaws.com/books/bhrihat_parasara_hora_shastra.pdf); [Sanskrit and translation of chapter 34](https://www.santkosh.com/library/books/brihat-parashara-hora-shastra/chapter-34); Paul Manley, *Birth Time Rectification: How to Find Your Exact Birth Time* (2022), supplied capture. The two public chapter-34 translations differ in their wording for node-yoga placement in 34.17; do not present a single broad node-yoga rule as universally settled. This experiment reports nodes' event links, not a new node-yoga status.

## Three non-additive diagnostics

For a favourable mapped event, evaluate three boolean flags separately for the main and subperiods in D1 or the event's own division: (1) a named formed yoga pair containing a running lord, with either pair member the direct matter-house lord; (2) a running node has at least one one-hop ruler-to-event link; (3) a running lord has the placement-plus-direct-link combination. Each flag counts an event once, regardless of duplicated planets, charts or indications. Pair formation, node links and combinations are displayed in the third period as descriptive evidence, but the third does not enter these flags. A false flag does not deny an event. Unfavourable or mixed events receive descriptive clues only; an explicit favourable outcome can make a normally mixed mapped matter eligible. Missing necessary frames make the result unavailable, not false. No flag replaces, adds points to, filters or ranks the existing birth-time candidates. The same functions and event outcome are used for dated readings, Validate, Rectify and every random date. No transit signal is added by these flags.

## Fresh public cases and controls

Select the five cases and the first two reference / third held-out events in the fixture without consulting model outputs. Record source reliability and dated-event assumptions. Priyanka's mother and grandmother have been studied previously, so cases are not wholly independent across studies. Reagan's time is disputed; include the two stated alternate-clock scenarios. Repeated weddings, births and elections are related observations, and Bolsonaro's held-out oath is the same transition as his 2018 election. Clock and zone uncertainty is explicitly recorded in the fixture; an invitation time is a sensitivity scenario, not a confirmed ceremony time.

At each reported time, assess the references and held-out event and six held-out comparison dates at ±60, ±120 and ±180 days, keeping their assumed clock and zone. They are not verified non-events and may be unrealistic. Compare each flag independently; report all ties. Run the app's seeded 200-set comparison for the two references if their span meets the app's one-year requirement. The third event can precede the references; held-out means its result was not used to select rules or cases, not prospective prediction. Also find the nearest D1 sign boundary within ±120 minutes of each reported time based only on geometry, and examine both sides 30 seconds from it. Preserve event-level flags, not just sums. A side matching a reported rising sign is only a reference, not a true-time label. Report each flag's matches, comparison-date similarity and pair-side changes without interpreting correlated measures as independent tests. Record full outputs and file hashes for reproducibility.

After that run, re-evaluate the user's previously supplied candidate times, events, event-time assumptions, and the month-wide November 2017 residence scenario without saving personal chart details in the repository. Retain old evidence and uncertainties. No candidate is confirmed or excluded by this small research diagnostic.

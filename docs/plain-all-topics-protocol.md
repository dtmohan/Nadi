# BNN and school-topic presentation review protocol

The purpose is to check whether plain explanations follow the existing calculation, preserve opposing or uncertain findings and remain navigable, not to prove astrology. All eleven BNN areas are considered. Children-related and health material is assessed for appropriate limits without trying to infer fertility, diagnosis, risk, or the presence or absence of a child. Money-related text is not treated as financial advice. Technical findings must remain available in the practitioner view. Areas held by the existing age gate remain held.

The Parashari, KP and Jaimini summaries are derived from each school's existing general-topic agreement voice, not the combined verdict. That adapter covers career, marriage, children, father and mother. It does not cover every rule or topic, does not add dated predictions, and does not currently map ALP to these general topics. A silent or unsupported rule must not become a negative prediction. Conditional and provisional classifications must be shown as such. Topic-specific and date-specific calculations remain in their original schools.

The four cases in `scripts/fixtures/plain-all-topics-fresh.json` were chosen before running this review, from different places and backgrounds, and do not occur in prior repository case fixtures. Birth times are as reported, not independently verified; Geeta Phogat's is recalled, and other publications give a different birth year for Yamina Benguigui. Compare the output with independent public biographies for selected life areas; retain misses and excessively broad interpretations as well as apparent matches. Events known only by year are not assigned invented dates. As a limited retrospective check, compare all selected BNN standing passages, including those behind the More passages disclosure, against the three dated public awards in the fixture, and against dates shifted ±180 days, all as viewed 90 days in advance. The shifted dates are not verified non-events. A passage overlapping any part of the local event day is a possible overlap only; event clocks are unknown. No personal chart or events are stored in this repository.

The source readings are not tuned to the outcomes and no event is inserted into the chart. Because the people and outcomes are known and the samples are small and selected, this cannot measure predictive accuracy. Changes informed by the review must be recorded; no user comprehension trial or independent practitioner review is implied.

The first public run exposed literal claims about a partner's behaviour and a parent's health, as well as astrological terminology in one standing summary. The plain presentation was revised to describe those topics cautiously and to keep the original wording in the source findings. No event or rule was changed, so the cases are not a clean holdout for the final wording.

During browser testing, a KP source link led to rules that stated a child's sex and family size as outcomes, conflicting with the new plain summary's limits. Those four rule descriptions are now qualified in the plain KP display and report; their original text remains in the practitioner view. This is a presentation change, not a change to the rules, their matching conditions or their polarity.

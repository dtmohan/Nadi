# Fresh event-catalogue integration check

This protocol and the four-case manifest were written before running the new catalogue
integration on the cases. The named people are absent from earlier structured Nadi chart
fixtures. They are a convenience sample of four public people in the United States, France
and Brazil, not a representative population sample. Birth times are those reported by
Astro-Databank and were not independently retrieved from the original certificates; an AA
label does not prove minute-level accuracy. Selected events are documented individually in
the manifest; a career selection announcement or qualification is not necessarily the date
of joining a job. Two birth-event dates rely on film/genealogical records, rather than inspected
original documents. The Holley wedding zone and several event clocks are unknown.

After the first run, the KP job registration was corrected because an independent-work
rule had been labelled as a job promise, and delivery-specific rules were removed from the
generic childbirth matter. This cohort is consequently discovery data, not the final holdout.

At each reported birth input, compare: the ordinary chart's KP findings, Parashari house/yoga
context and BNN/Jaimini/Prasna contextual projections with the selected-event API and Validate;
compare ALP findings with the ordinary ALP evaluator at the exact event instant. Recast one
selectable Rectify midpoint per subject and compare all new evidence. For events at the reported
birth time, evaluate the same matter at offsets of −180, −120, −60, +60, +120 and +180 days,
retaining each event's specified or assumed clock and zone. Compare evidence IDs, ALP topic
findings and existing event scores; these alternatives are not confirmed non-events.

Where two events span at least one year, inspect the first reproducible random-date set and
replay it directly. Report a comparison as unavailable when the app does not generate one.
Record all results, including ties, missed registration and disagreement. Do not adjust weights
or select people or event dates after inspecting results. Period-lord links and broad natal
house or area associations are descriptive, not a new event scoring formula. Equivalence across
paths validates software consistency, not astrological prediction or a birth time. Personal
chart inputs and results are not included in this public fixture or report.

# Public case protocol for the 2026 reliability corrections

This is an engineering and exploratory case check, not a test that astrology
predicts events or can recover birth times. The four cases below were selected
before running these corrected calculations. Their names were absent from the
existing Nadi fixture files. They cover people born in the United States,
France and Morocco, with careers in entertainment, public life and visual art.
The cases are public figures, not a representative population sample.

Astro-Databank attributes each recorded birth time to a certificate or birth
record, but Nadi has not examined the original documents. Some clocks are
rounded. Additional published sources are listed for each event; they may
share an underlying report and may not independently confirm every precise
detail. Event times and even locations are
unavailable in some cases; the manifest says when noon or a time zone is
assumed. Jeanne-Claude's published wedding dates disagree by years, so the
wedding is excluded. Her single event cannot support a random-date baseline.

For each person, scan ±10 minutes around the reported birth time, comparing the
four existing source-scoped experimental measures. Re-evaluate the same event
at four nearby dates and, where possible, compare 200 reproducible random-date
sets with the same eligibility, clock and zone assumptions. Verify that chart
topic reading, Validate and the Rectify midpoint share event conditions and the
same comparison totals. No rule weights are tuned to these cases. Report the
percentage of the scanned window tied for the top midpoint score; sampled
intervals and random dates are not independently established negative events.
These percentages and ranks are not birth-time probabilities.

Reproduce: `TZ=UTC node --import tsx scripts/eval-qualified-event-fresh.ts scripts/fixtures/reliability-fresh-cases.json`.

For the three dated awards, the limited selected BNN standing briefing overlapped **0 of 3 event days** and **0 of 6 shifted comparison days**. These are only the current and next selected passages, viewed 90 days before each date; this does not test every transit or every method. A shifted date is not a verified non-event, and a day overlap would not by itself establish that an award was predicted. The events and people are known, selected and too few to support an accuracy claim.

The separate school summaries reproduce each selected general-topic voice and retain its conditional/provisional status. They are not a fresh validation of the underlying Parashari, KP or Jaimini rules. The four public examples exposed awkward or overly definite language, which was revised as recorded in the protocol. They were not used to infer anyone's health, fertility, family size or birth time. A separate private comparison uses the user's own supplied events.

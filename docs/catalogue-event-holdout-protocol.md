# Final holdout for shared catalogue event context

The first run was inspected before a display-only qualified-indication summary, four additional
unrelated event-matter registrations, unique IDs for two duplicated illness rules, descriptive
ALP/Parashari period-participant comparison frequencies, and a precedence correction to that
new summary were added.
The recorded KP and Parashari score weights were not tuned, but a rerun after these edits is an
**exploratory reanalysis**, not a fully untouched or blinded final holdout.

Prepared after the scope correction identified in the earlier discovery run and before this
holdout was evaluated. The four chart subjects in `scripts/fixtures/catalogue-event-holdout.json`
do not appear in earlier structured Nadi chart fixtures or the discovery cohort. They cover
France, India, Brazil and the United States, including a politician, sign-language interpreter,
and actors. This is a small convenience sample with correlated events and uneven source quality,
not a representative or blinded accuracy study.

Use the same reproducible procedure specified in `catalogue-event-fresh-protocol.md`: equality
across the ordinary chart, selected-date reading, Validate, the candidate midpoint and the first
random-date set; six fixed-offset dates per event; and a ±5-minute candidate scan with all ties.
Report event clocks, assumptions, mixed outcomes, score ties and cases where a random comparison
is unavailable. The 09:30 Celina Jaitly birth input is a midpoint of a reported hour, not a
recorded or precise time; the 2012 twins' date has a one-day disagreement in later reporting.
Two Michelle Bolsonaro marriage ceremonies refer to the same couple and are not independent.
Édouard Philippe's 2010 mayoral date differs by one day between sources, and his 14:30 2017
announcement is a schedule, not an independently checked instant. The public birth records
are not personally inspected. Exact parity is a software check; historical date comparisons
and ranking within a chosen narrow scan do not prove astrology or birth-time recovery.

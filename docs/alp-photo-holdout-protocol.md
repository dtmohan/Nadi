# Printed ALP marriage page: fresh holdout protocol

The user provided two photographs of their printed ALP textbook after the first four-person cohort had been scored. The page headed chapter 28, “Timing the Marriage Through ALP,” says the natal and progressed seventh lords connect with the navamsa lagna. It gives an example of both lords connecting with dasa/bhukti and a female-chart example in which the progressed seventh lord is the dasa or bhukti lord. The source does not define all forms of “connection,” explicitly distinguish natal from progressed navamsa lagna, or prescribe a full natal-promise gate. The available online volume 2 transcription gives a stricter two-lord period example. Treat these as variants, not equivalent source wording.

Before running the calculations on this new cohort, freeze the following observations: pada-navamsa clue, exact natal/progressed seventh-lord dasa-bhukti pair in either order, progressed seventh lord in either period (an explicitly marked generalisation of one printed example), and each seventh lord's D9 sign equalling (a) natal D9 ascendant or (b) the D9 sign of the progressed ALP point. Same-sign contact is one narrow app interpretation; it does not settle every textual connection. No fact is an event denial, a combined event score, or a completed natal-promise gate. BPHS 18.1's spouse-happiness clue is separate.

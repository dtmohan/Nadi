# Aurora KP research adapter: frozen validation protocol

This protocol evaluates an experimental Nadi operationalization of Leena Aurora, *The Ultimate Guide to KP Astrology Techniques* (2024), supplied Kindle capture pp. 252–261, 266–268, 335–341, 438–439, 571–572 and 634–637. It does not claim that the source or the rules predict events or establish birth times. The implementation and this protocol were fixed before looking at the evaluation outputs. Public cases were chosen for traceable birth and event reports, not representativeness; event and birth sources are not assumed infallible.

## Fixed adapter

- Supported matters: first marriage, childbirth/family expansion, employment or promotion, and business. Other matters are not evaluated by this adapter. Career adds the sixth cusp for employment and the seventh for business to the tenth cusp; marriage uses the seventh, childbirth the fifth. Promotion and employment still share a category: the adapter does not claim to model every promotion-specific exception.
- Self-significators: a body whose ruled stars contain no natal body; a body in its own star; or both bodies in a mutual star exchange. The source's 1959 litigation and 1983 education examples reproduce its stated lists, while the Rahu example reproduces its four cusp sub links. Additional self-significator cusp-star/sub associations are visible separately.
- Script layers use Nadi's existing four-step signification plus self-significator cusp-star/sub associations. If a planet is in its own star, the adapter uses its sub and sub-sub at the script's star and sub levels. Extending these cusp links to every applicable layer and substituting Nadi's four-step houses are explicit app conventions; the book does not provide an unambiguous mechanical mapping for all cases. Node aspects and other variants are not modeled as exhaustive.
- A stage passes only if its star and sub script layers each touch at least one supporting house and neither touches an adverse house. Mixed, adverse and silent stages do not pass; later stages cannot rescue a failed predecessor. This is a deliberately strict research screen, not a source-prescribed universal threshold. It leaves the book's nuanced mixed outcomes unresolved.
- Cusp stages precede major, sub and sub-sub periods. The model reports only a full chain as a supported event. It does not add partial points, use transit contacts as a gate, or invent fourth/fifth-period precision from date-only events. The period-lord, Sun and Moon event skies are descriptive. The app keeps this method separate from its existing KP and other scores.

## Tests

1. **Conformance:** reproduce named source examples for self-significators, cusp links, number-table entries and reported sky where the inputs permit it. A matched source example checks arithmetic/interpretation against that example, not independent event prediction.
2. **Fresh cases:** use the documented first marriage and children in the manifest, with reported local birth time and zone, KP ayanamsa and mean nodes. Report birth source, conflicts, locations and event-date/time assumptions. Related people/events are not independent samples. One case with only one usable event may be reported as a descriptive example, not a held-out test.
3. **Time scan:** sample every two minutes from 30 minutes before to 30 minutes after each reported anchor, including the anchor. For competing AM/PM accounts scan both windows separately. Score each sample by the number of full chains in alternating chronological training dates. Report the count of tied best *sampled points*, the score at the reported anchor and whether any strictly positive top exists. Only when the positive top varies across samples, select the closest-to-anchor top point as a diagnostic and score held-out dates there and at the anchor. Ties, rounded records and the two-minute grid prevent a precise birth-time claim. A sample fraction is not exact interval coverage.
4. **Controls:** at each reported anchor, use the same event categories and times/zones, the same evaluator and 200 reproducible randomly selected comparison-date sets across the actual event-date span. Report full-chain counts and the comparison mean. Random dates may include real undisclosed events, do not preserve plausible age/order, and are not verified negatives. Date-only events use noon in their documented local zone. Repeat date-only event checks at 00:00 and 23:59 local time; changing a result marks it time-sensitive, not proof about when the event occurred.
5. **Personal case:** use only the user's supplied chart and event details, in memory or a temporary file, without adding them to repository fixtures. Compare previously discussed candidate times and report unresolved stages, actual-versus-random checks and any assumptions. Do not tune on the personal chart, save it, or use a top score as verification of birth time.

No threshold, category, candidate time, source conflict or adverse result is changed after seeing a result. If the source examples do not specify a decision, leave it unresolved and report it. Stop short of interpreting this adapter as a complete or validated Aurora method.

# Functional lordship: fixed exploratory protocol

This protocol was written before evaluating the fresh public cases or the personal comparison requested in the conversation. It is a source-informed engineering experiment, not a published scoring formula or evidence that astrology can recover a birth time. Preserve all results, including ties and adverse results; do not retune the rule using these cases.

## Evidence

For each event already mapped by the Manley evidence engine (residence D4, childbirth D7, marriage D9, employment/promotion D10, education D24), show the main, sub and third Vimshottari period planets in D1 and that event's division. For each classical planet in each frame show the whole-sign houses it owns, its occupied sign and house, limited sign dignity and the existing BPHS 34 rising-sign classification from `LAGNA_NATURE`. Apply the same classification table to the divisional ascendant as an explicitly provisional application convention; BPHS's natal rising-sign table is not claimed to prescribe this entire divisional experiment. Preserve listed favourable, malefic, neutral and yogakaraka classifications as contextual descriptors, not event verdicts. If a planet is only named in a two-planet yoga pair, show its partner and the app's conjunction/full-aspect relationship separately; do not promote it to an independently favourable yogakaraka. If the table has no applicable classification, say unclassified. Lunar nodes own no houses in this convention and are unclassified; do not silently assign their dispositors' classification. A planet can have several lordships; show all of them.

The Manley primary/supporting links, D1 and divisional house assessments, dignity, conjunctions and other difficulties remain separate. Do not interpret an exalted sign as full strength or automatic cancellation, or treat a functional malefic as incapable of positive results. Do not use an incomplete D1 condition to suppress the division. The third period is displayed with earlier periods, not an independent confirmation or a third point. Explicitly unfavourable and mixed events, or matters whose default nature is not favourable, receive descriptive evidence only unless the user records a favourable outcome. Job loss, vehicles, foreign travel and all other unmapped matters do not get an invented event-house/division rule. No transit condition is implemented by this new functional-lordship diagnostic.

## Narrow diagnostic for a fair comparison

For a favourable, mapped event, count the **distinct** main- and subperiod classical planets which directly own the mapped matter house in D1 or the relevant division and are separately classified as auspicious or yogakaraka in the same frame. Count a planet once even if it appears at both levels or qualifies in both frames. Conditional yoga-pair membership alone, neutral/malefic/unclassified planets, nodes, and mere aspect or occupancy do not score in this deliberately narrow subset. A count of zero means the subset has no such direct lord; it is not an adverse event prediction, denial of promise, or proof that the time is wrong. These 0–2 counts are a transparent measurement convention, not a BPHS or Manley point formula. Do not add them to v1–v3 or use them to filter or select a candidate birth time. The same evaluator and outcome eligibility must be used for actual and Monte Carlo dates. Existing Manley chronological period evidence remains visible, and the third-period display is not conditioned or scored as a new stage.

Narasimha Rao, *Vedic Astrology: An Integrated Approach*, functional nature](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf#page=179) and [periods and divisional matters](https://www.vedicastrologer.org/articles/vedic_astro_textbook.pdf#page=226); Paul Manley, *Birth Time Rectification* (2022), capture pp. 64–67 and 123–133, on considering the D1 lord and divisional placements. None prescribes the diagnostic count or guarantees an outcome.

## Fresh public-case audit

Preselect five documented public cases in `scripts/fixtures/parashari-functional-fresh.json`, none used as charts in earlier fixtures: Bill Gates, Joe Biden, Diana, Princess of Wales, Yuvraj Singh and Céline Dion. Two named events per person are designated reference events and the third is held out. Diana's son William appeared as a chart in an earlier study, so his birth is not an independent historical event from that work. Record date and time bases, alternate birth reports, event sources and any conflicting event date. A small convenience sample and correlated events from the same family cannot establish accuracy; Rodden ratings and birth-record quotations are source descriptions, not independent certification. For figures with material clock conflicts, run the explicitly named sensitivity cases without treating one report as confirmed.

Before any results were calculated, an initial fresh-fixture guard rejected Einstein, Indira Gandhi, Barack Obama and Sachin Tendulkar because they appeared in earlier general validation fixtures. They were replaced by the four cases above. The diagnostic rule, controls and selection procedure were not changed in response to model results.

At each reported time, calculate each reference and held-out event and compare the held-out date with dates ±60, ±120 and ±180 days, retaining its clock and zone. These controls are not verified non-events; some can be implausible ages or circumstances. On the two reference events also report the app's 200 random-date comparison using the same diagnostic. The app deliberately omits this comparison when the reference dates span less than one year; record it as unavailable rather than substituting a score. This existing eligibility condition was documented when the first run stopped on Diana's shorter span, before that run produced any output. For each case, locate the closest D1 rising-sign boundary within ±120 minutes of the reported instant, independently of event scores, and calculate both sides 30 seconds from the boundary. Preserve complete period and frame evidence, including changed divisions; both sides use their own exact natal planets and period boundaries. Report which side shares the published rising sign, reference counts and held-out counts for **both** sides, including ties. If no boundary is in the range, record that explicitly. Since the published birth time may be rounded, disputed or incorrect, the side with its calculated sign is a reference label, not ground truth. Pair comparisons do not validate the count or prove the reported time. Do not choose a side on a tie, fit weights, or treat related event dates as independent observations.

## Personal comparison

After the frozen fresh-case run, examine the previously discussed 06:51–06:58 window and both sides of the calculated Libra–Scorpio boundary using the user's supplied birth settings and dated events. Include eligible events on their own terms; explicitly list what is unmapped and how any missing event time, date or zone is handled. Month-only events require a month-wide sensitivity check or must remain qualitative, never an invented date treated as fact. Compare the per-event evidence and diagnostic counts, not just totals, and preserve contradictory and unresolved indications. The two sides of the D1 sign boundary must not be extrapolated to all other Scorpio or Libra times, because divisional ascendants and period boundaries can change within either sign. Never save the user's chart or life events in the repository.

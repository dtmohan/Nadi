# Exploratory Moon-based gochara comparison

This protocol, the public cases, and the evaluation rule are fixed before running the comparison. It asks whether the app's **existing** Moon-based transit reading provides useful discrimination between documented event dates and nearby comparison dates, and whether it improves separation as a tie-breaker for the existing event scores. It does not validate astrology, calibrate probabilities, prove that a comparison date was a non-event, or establish a birth time.

## Basis and scope

Phaladeepika 26.1 identifies the natal Moon as the principal transit reference, and 26.2 gives general favourable positions. See [26.1](https://www.siva.sh/phaladeepika/26/1) and [26.2](https://www.siva.sh/phaladeepika/26/2). These are general transit judgments, not a published numerical formula for these particular events, KP, or rectification. The app's full verdict additionally applies its provisional Ashtakavarga threshold and ordering, vedha, dignity/combustion, and aspects; see `shared/gochara.ts`. These effects are not independent of each other. In particular, the Ashtakavarga contribution can vary with the natal ascendant. The effective portion of a sign and several further transit techniques are displayed or documented but not applied to the verdict.

## Fixed cases and comparisons

Use every event in `scripts/fixtures/moon-gochara-fresh.json`: publicly documented cases not used in previous structured Nadi research fixtures. Birth times use the cited Astro-Databank records and are not independently certified by this experiment; record conflicts and historical time standards. Cases were selected for published birth records and corroborated events, rather than randomly drawn from a population. There is more than one event per person for some people; report the number of people and events and the person-weighted as well as event-weighted mean.

For each event, draw 200 **distinct** calendar dates uniformly without replacement from offsets -365 through -8 and +8 through +365 days. The generator, seed and implementation are part of the audit script. Omit an invalid or ambiguous local event time and draw the next eligible date. Use the same local clock and timezone as the event on each sampled date; date-only events default to noon. Controls outside the person's lifetime are excluded. They are comparison dates, not verified non-events. A score of 50% is the middle of this comparison distribution; it is not a probability of occurrence or accuracy. Use midranks so half the tied dates are counted as below the event.

## Predeclared measurements

1. Primary exploratory feature: count Jupiter and Saturn whose **full app verdict** is `favourable` (0–2). Equal weighting and counting are research choices, not claims that a classical text gives a combined event score. Report the event's midrank among its comparison dates and the percentage of sampled dates at each value.
2. Evaluate the same planets with the unmodified favourable-house flag as a separate feature; report the two planets' individual actual positions and full verdicts. As an exploratory secondary feature only, count favourable full verdicts for all seven classical planets. Do not substitute the house flag for the full verdict.
3. For the KP existing period/cusp score, the existing KP transit score, and the existing Parashari divisional v3 score, report their separate midranks; repeat with the primary 0–2 feature used strictly as a lexicographic tie-breaker. Do not add a combined score to production or claim that the feature is an established KP rule. These comparisons ask whether it orders equal-scored dates differently; they cannot establish that this helps forecasting without verified non-events and independent prospective data.
4. For each event without a confirmed clock time, recompute the same day's transit at 00:00, 06:00, 12:00 and 18:00 local time, noting changes in the transiting Moon sign, two-planet verdict/count, and seven-planet count. Exact reported times remain as reported. Evaluate the stated alternate birth/event data without selecting an alternative after looking at scores.
5. Compare calculation output with the application's Validate path on actual events; both use the event's actual instant and their respective ayanamsas. Assess the user's candidate birth times in a separate stdin-only run; never save the user's birth data or events in this public fixture, results, or repository.

Time, source quality, historical time zones, unspecified event clocks, seasonality, slow-moving transits, event correlations, missing events on comparison dates and small convenience-sample selection all limit inference. These are retrospective, exploratory measurements with several secondary checks, not a prospective or independently replicated test. No numerical result is a probability of a birth time or prediction.

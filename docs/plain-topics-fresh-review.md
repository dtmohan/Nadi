# Career and marriage: plain-reading review

The new cards explain selected Nadi findings in everyday terms, expose their original findings, and show selected Jupiter and Saturn passages without calling them event predictions. The calculations and transit selection have not been changed. The [protocol](plain-topics-fresh-protocol.md) describes the checks, limits and changes made while reviewing cases; the fixtures and JSON outputs contain the birth-data, biography and dated-event sources.

## Public cases

The cases were selected in three successive sets. Their reported birth times have varying reliability. The people and their outcomes were known, and wording was refined after seeing some of the outputs, so these results are not a blinded or clean final-wording holdout.

| Case | What the career card says compared with the documented career | Dated events: possible overlap with a selected passage | Shifted dates: possible overlaps |

| Toni Morrison | Writing appears in a long list of alternatives, alongside finance, law and other fields. | Nobel announcement: no; the next Jupiter passage starts five days later. | 1 of 2 |

| Werner Heisenberg | Technical and research work appear among the alternatives. | Marriage: yes; Nobel presentation: yes. | 1 of 4 |

| Simone Biles | The card does not identify gymnastics. | Olympic gold: yes; marriage: no. | 1 of 4 |

Across the three sets, 5 of 11 dated events and 6 of 22 dates shifted by 180 days overlapped a selected passage. The shifted dates are **not verified non-events**. They show that broad windows can cover comparison dates too; this small, selected, retrospective exercise cannot establish accuracy or a false-positive rate. An overlap with part of the local event day is treated as possible because the event clock is unknown. No overlap means only that the limited current/next briefing did not display an overlapping passage; it is not a verdict on all Nadi rules or the other schools.

These results check that the displayed text is traceable to existing findings and give examples of substantive misses. They do not show that readers understood the cards: that requires a separate user comprehension study. Practitioner readings and detailed findings remain available, including where methods disagree.

Run the individual sets using the commands in the protocol.

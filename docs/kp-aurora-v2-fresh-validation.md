# Aurora mixed-house adapter v2: fresh-case results

**Finding:** the v2 adapter reproduces selected house coordinates and one worked decision from the supplied book, but did not pass this small fresh-case test. At the four principal reported birth times it supported **0 of 12** publicly dated marriage and birth events as complete staged chains. Every sampled training window had a top score of zero at every point. It did not select a time or demonstrate that it can distinguish real events from comparison dates. These are failures of this adapter as an event-ranking instrument, not evidence that the reported events failed to happen.

The fixed rules and evaluation plan are in [kp-aurora-v2-protocol.md](kp-aurora-v2-protocol.md). The exact cases and links are in [kp-aurora-v2-fresh-cases.json](../scripts/fixtures/kp-aurora-v2-fresh-cases.json); the full machine-readable results are in [kp-aurora-v2-fresh-results.json](kp-aurora-v2-fresh-results.json). Run `node --import tsx scripts/eval-kp-aurora-v2.ts` from the project root to reproduce them.

## Source examples and boundaries

The source is Leena Aurora, *The Ultimate Guide to KP Astrology Techniques* (2024), supplied Kindle capture pages 260–262, 360–365, 462–474 and 582–599. Numbers are capture pages, not necessarily printed page numbers. Source-conformance tests use the author's printed longitudes and cusp data, not her chart reconstructed from a different ephemeris.

- For the 1983 chart, direct occupied/owned-house coordinates for Saturn, Mars, Venus, Jupiter, Rahu and Ketu reproduce the numbers she prints. In the 2013 childbirth example, a mixed star with supportive 11 and adverse 8 proceeds with caution when the sub supports 2 and 5 and has no adverse house. The source's stated Saturn/Rahu/Jupiter chain is supported in the transcription test.
- Recomputing the natal Moon from the source's stated birth details with Nadi's KP ephemeris differs from the printed Moon by about 65 arcseconds and gives Saturn/Rahu/Saturn on the event date. That crosses a period boundary, so the printed example is not reproduced end to end from the birth details. The source does not supply sufficient settings to explain the discrepancy.
- The separate 1970 chart's node coordinates reproduce the author's printed numbers for the 1988 marriage example. Its cusp passes with caution, but the author proceeds when a later sub carries both supporting and adverse houses. V2 conservatively leaves that stage unresolved. This is a known disagreement with the author, not a validation of that conservative choice. The 2022 job discussion also weighs a mixed sub in context.
- Nadi's full-sign node aspects, uniform promotion of self-significators and extension of the repeated-star/sub treatment are documented app conventions. They are not claimed to be a complete book algorithm. Only the first three Vimshottari period lords are staged; the event-day transit positions are displayed but do not trigger or score the result. The general job category is not a specific theory of promotion or job change.

## New public cases

The four people below were not used in the earlier Aurora or existing project validation fixtures. Multiple reports can repeat a common underlying claim: dates and reported birth times are not treated as error-free. The text of the birth records was not examined directly. At each anchor we used 31 samples, two minutes apart across ±30 minutes; chronological alternating event dates were allocated to training and holdout before scoring. No candidate was selected because all training scores were zero and flat. Thus the five held-out dates were evaluated at their reported anchors but **not** at a fitted candidate.

At the principal anchors, 10 events stopped at the cusp stage; Rihanna's two events passed the cusp with caution but stopped at the major period. Branson's first wedding date is also listed in Astro-Databank, and Holly's date appears in a [second secondary listing](https://www.dicytrends.com/holly-branson-everything-about-richard-bransons-daughter/); the independence of the underlying claims is unknown. Their event clock times and locations were not established, so the UK time zone and local noon were assumed. Rihanna's children's times are attributed by public reports to birth certificates, but those certificates were not inspected here. Other unknown event clocks were set to local noon; the fixtures record the event time zones and sources. India observed UTC+06:30 on Bachchan's birth date and the historical time zone was used.

At all seven anchor windows the 200 reproducible random-date sets also averaged zero complete chains, ranging from 0 to 0. The controls preserve each event's clock and time zone, but the random dates are not known non-events; the zero result therefore cannot establish specificity. In a midnight/noon/23:59 sensitivity check, Branson's 1981 event at the 07:00 anchor changed a raw stage signature across the day but not its complete-chain result. KP true nodes or Lahiri instead of KP ayanamsa changed some stage signatures, while none of those variants produced a complete chain at the anchors. The coordinates perturbation of ±0.05 degrees at the fixed birth instant did not change the stage signatures in this sample. These sensitivity comparisons were exploratory and did not modify the frozen rule.

This is a small and event-skewed test: the 12 events are three first marriages and nine births, not independent observations from 12 people; only one subject has a reportedly recorded birth time, and several other times or event details are uncertain. It cannot resolve whether stricter thresholds, house profiles, calculation conventions, documented time uncertainty, or the premise itself account for the failures. It does show that this version should remain labeled research and should not be used to choose a birth time. No personal chart or events are included in the fixtures.

# ALP boundaries, KP legacy scores and dignity: fresh-case protocol

This convenience sample and its event selection were written before the corrected results were run. It is a software and source-fidelity check, not an assessment of astrology's predictive validity or a demonstration that birth times can be recovered.

Use the four people and the dates in `scripts/fixtures/alp-dignity-fresh.json`. Check fixture names against all earlier fixture files. The sample intentionally includes different places, occupations and periods, but is small and selected for publicly available birth and family data, not representativeness. Report disagreements in dates and birth-time sources; do not select an alternative because it improves a score. Twins count as one delivery event. Do not use the user's personal chart or events as public fixtures.

For each case use Lahiri, mean nodes, and the documented time basis. Compare the precise anchor, the selectable midpoint of the ordinary segment containing it, and the independent ALP band containing it. Scan ±10 minutes with the 200 seeded random-date sets. Independently run Validate at the band midpoint; require ALP actual and comparison statistics to agree. Require the legacy KP aggregate and comparison to agree between Validate and Rectify at the same candidate. Report per-event ALP and KP research conditions, and raw-score coverage on the tested whole-second grid, without presenting a winner as the true birth time. Ordinary non-ALP segments remain midpoint samples. If a comparison cannot be computed, report it unavailable, never zero.

For a fixed sensitivity check, recompute Fonda's wedding on Nov 30; Gossuin's first twins on Dec 20, second twins on Oct 11, and her whole chart at the independently described speculative 02:30. These are source discrepancies, not alternative fitted results. For each event also inspect dates 60 and 180 days before and after, when valid. These controls are not known non-events.

Event dates are retrospective and most event clocks are unknown, so noon in the indicated zone is assumed. Comparison dates are not established non-events and can be implausible ages or out of order; a mid-rank is descriptive, not a probability or a significance test after candidate selection. Astro-Databank and Astrotheme's birth-record classifications are secondary reports; the records themselves were not inspected. Rounded times, conflicts, historical local mean time and jurisdictional conversions limit any birth-time inference. A child's birth may be reported by multiple outlets repeating a common source.

The dignity checks are source-scoped: inspect which sign and degree facts actually feed each school, test the corrected overlaps and missing conditions with synthetic counterexamples, and report rules still using sign-level conventions. Do not claim that a traditional rule is empirically validated because the program implements it consistently.

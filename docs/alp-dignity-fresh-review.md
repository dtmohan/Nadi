# Fresh public-case check: ALP, legacy KP and dignity

The corrected code and the public-only [predeclared protocol](alp-dignity-validation-protocol.md) were tested on four previously unused people, 11 dated events and five wedding events. The [full machine-readable results](alp-dignity-fresh-results.json) contain source links, 200-date baselines, alternate dates, controls and code hashes. Twin deliveries count once. These are consistency and exploratory checks, not prospective evidence that astrology predicts events or an independent verification of birth records.

The selected ALP marriage-pada condition matched **none of the five wedding dates at the reported birth anchors**. This is a counterexample to treating this partial condition as necessary for marriage; it does not establish that the complete ALP method is false. The source also requires a natal promise, which this test does not establish. The documented times and event dates are uncertain, and the five weddings are not independent random draws.

The ALP raw maxima show how much of each tested grid ties; they do not imply a correct time is among the maxima. Fonda's wedding is reported as November 29 by [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000403/) and [Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/education/news-wires-white-papers-and-books/fonda-bridget-1964), but November 30 by Astro-Databank. Both dates yield 0 for this ALP clue at 03:45. Gossuin's 02:30 speculative alternative yields 1 for her wedding, while the 02:00 anchor gives 0; neither result resolves the competing birth-time sources. Pedro's [in-person wedding in Rio](https://dpedroiv.parquesdesintra.pt/en/chronology/1817/november/6/pedro-marries-leopoldine-josepha-caroline--archduc/53) followed a [marriage by proxy in Vienna](https://atom.itamaraty.gov.br/not-196-1-8-1817-09-18-01); this study uses the in-person ceremony. Historical local mean time and unrecorded event clocks add uncertainty.

At the preselected four nearby control dates per wedding, only one of 20 ALP checks matched; these dates are not known non-events, and adjacent checks are correlated. All event times were assumed noon. Legacy KP totals and their comparison distributions use one natal cusp point for repeated identical matter/cusp/house combinations and three per-event period checks. In this sample every KP mid-rank was below the app's exploratory 95th-percentile highlighting cutoff. The maxima and mid-ranks are not calibrated confidence levels, and the random dates need not preserve the order or plausible ages of events.

The event-at-time ordinary readings, Validate and Rectify were compared at the same candidate instants. The ALP band's actual score and its entire 200-set summary matched Validate at its midpoint, including the historical local-mean-time case. Legacy KP totals and baselines matched at the common ordinary-segment midpoint. This does not imply all natal dignity rules feed every event score: registration is explicit. At the four anchors, the revised Prasna 14.40 sign proxy left at least one of the fifth-, seventh- or tenth-house checks unresolved in every chart. None of the three specifically revised BNN dignity rules fired at these anchors; their positive and negative conditions are covered by synthetic tests, not demonstrated by this public sample.

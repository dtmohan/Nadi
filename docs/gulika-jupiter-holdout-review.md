# Jupiter–Gulika aspect: fresh placement-first case review

The [protocol](gulika-jupiter-holdout-protocol.md) fixed the selection and two comparison groups
before these family outcomes were sought. From the remaining 55 fifth-house cases in the same
Astro-Databank C sample, 14 had Jupiter's fifth/ninth full sign aspect and 41 did not. The
predefined country quota yielded **nine** aspect cases and ten without the fifth/ninth aspect;
all were retained and investigated. The quota, not the family outcome, caused the aspect-group
shortfall. The [screen](gulika-jupiter-holdout-screen.json) and
[computed sensitivities](gulika-jupiter-holdout-results.json) contain all cases.

This is an exploratory holdout after earlier research on the same one-letter source. It is not
an independently preregistered or blinded population study. It selects on a calculated fifth
house, AA source rating, time stability at ±5 minutes and public biography availability; none of
these establishes the actual birth time. The cases are not matched on opportunity or desire to
have children, and there is no representative non-fifth control group. A documented parent is
not a fertility assessment. Non-parenthood and biological non-parenthood cannot be inferred
from silence, an unmarried status or the absence of children from an obituary.

## Source and interpretation

**Correction:** the passage previously labelled 19.68 in this project was **18.68**. Raman's
[Prasna Marga II, chapter 18.68](https://pdfcoffee.com/prasna-marga-ii-pdf-free.html) discusses
a trinal Jupiter–Gulika relationship as one positive indication for a children question. The
associated notes explicitly use a question chart for one of the other calculations, and the
worked example asks for multiple favourable factors. Applying the Jupiter relationship to a
birth chart is an exploratory extrapolation; it is not a demonstrated cancellation of 14.67–70.
This review uses Jupiter's **fifth/ninth full sign aspects** as its primary trine comparison;
same-sign conjunction and the seventh aspect are separate.

The new factual comparison of Gulika with the natal Kshetra sphuta is a limited same-sign
reading of **19.8**. Its Gulika sentence follows a Kshetra discussion; applying it to Beeja is
an exploratory extension, not an explicit statement of the verse. The verse discusses both
association and aspect; the app does not implement an aspect *cast by* Gulika, and the source's
nature and outcome language must not be turned into a fertility prediction. The app retains its existing ghati-based primary sphuta
calculation from 19.5–9. The fifth-house benefic occupancy and aspect lists use Nadi's BPHS-based
natural-benefic and full-sign-aspect conventions; Prasna Marga 19.27 discusses fifth-house
affliction and absence of benefic aspect, but a single displayed fact is not that complete rule.

The JSON contains ±15/±5-minute samples and both Beeja and Kshetra points as descriptive
calculations, not a claim about a person's body. No individual certificate was independently
examined for this review. Astro-Databank's AA rating and source notes are its reported record
provenance; links are supplied so the source and possible conflicting accounts can be examined.

## What is and is not tested

Charlot, Culp and Copperfield are documented parents whose app-calculated Beeja shares
Gulika's sign; Caprino is a documented parent whose Kshetra shares the sign. This checks the
**calculation and coexistence with parenthood**, not the vague source claim of a particular
kind of trouble, Gulika's unspecified aspect, or fertility. No predictive weight is inferred.

For a fifth-house Gulika, Jupiter's fifth/ninth aspect on the point is also an aspect on the
same fifth **sign**. These are two descriptions of overlapping evidence, not independent votes.
Neither new clue changes KP/Parashari event, rectification or random-date scores. A useful
future study would fix a complete combination and definitions first, include reliable
documented non-parents as well as parents, and separately validate an actual dated horary
question. Run `node --import tsx scripts/eval-gulika-jupiter-holdout.ts` to reproduce the factors.
